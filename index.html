What is RAID?

RAID is a data storage technology that combines multiple physical disks into one logical unit. It improves performance, reliability and availability of data, and it is widely used in servers, data centers, cloud storage and enterprise systems.

  • Disks can fail at any time.
  • Systems need high speed and large storage.
  • Protect data and keep the system running.
Not a backup

RAID protects against disk failure. It does not protect against accidental deletion, malware, ransomware, human error, or fire and floods. Always use a separate backup strategy.

Where RAID is used

Data centers

High availability and uptime for cloud and web services.

Enterprise storage

Business applications and large databases.

Cloud storage

Scalable, reliable storage on platforms like AWS, Azure and Google Cloud.

Media and entertainment

Video editing, streaming and fast data access.

Healthcare

Patient records, imaging and data protection with reliability.

Conclusion

  • RAID improves speed, reliability and data availability.
  • Different levels suit different needs and budgets.
  • RAID 5 and 6 offer good balance of performance and safety.
  • RAID 10 is best for high performance and fault tolerance.
  • Always plan according to your data needs, budget and risk level.
